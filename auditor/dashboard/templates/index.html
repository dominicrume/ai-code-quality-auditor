How to read these

What the numbers are, and are not

Each run reports five metrics. Three of them are properties of the code alone and any linter could compute them. The one that matters here is scope drift, which needs a specification and therefore cannot be computed by a tool that does not know what was asked for.

A reading is taken at a commit and dated. Two corrections to published figures are recorded as errata in the repository, and the figures they correct are kept beside the corrected ones rather than replaced.
