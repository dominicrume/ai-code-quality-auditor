# Protocol deviations

## Deviation 001 — 2026-05-31

**What changed:** For replit_agent and antigravity conditions, the
10 reps per (spec × vendor) cell were generated by replaying one
captured session 10 times rather than re-running the vendor's web/
desktop IDE 10 times.

**Why:** Both vendors are browser/desktop-bound with no scriptable
CLI. 10 hand-run sessions per cell × 6 cells = 60 manual captures =
intractable within the 5-day collection window.

**Analytical consequence:** Within-cell variance for these two
conditions is structurally zero by construction. Reported as N=1
effective per cell; the 10× listing is for CSV-shape consistency
with the other three conditions. Statistical tests treat these
cells as singletons (excluded from ANOVA variance estimates,
included in ranked comparisons).

## Deviation 002 — 2026-05-31

**What changed:** Two web-IDE × spec cells deferred to a follow-up session:
- replit_agent × internal_tool_cli
- antigravity × data_pipeline

**Why:** Within-session capacity. The browser/desktop IDE flow for these
vendors is hands-on and the four other cells (replit × education,
replit × pipeline, antigravity × education, antigravity × cli) were
prioritised to keep all five conditions represented in the headline CSV.

**Analytical consequence:** Those two cells report N=0 in run_001. Pairwise
comparisons involving them are excluded for this run. Full 6/6 coverage
planned for run_003 once the human_control sessions also land.

**Closing note, added 2026-10-22.** This entry was never closed and, read on
its own, it contradicts the dataset. Both cells were captured on 1 June 2026;
`data/reports/main_001.provenance.json` timestamps the complete 4 x 3 x 10
matrix, and `main_001.csv` carries ten rows in each of them. The
`replit_agent x internal_tool_cli` cell is the source of the study's headline
qualitative finding (section 4.3.1), so the sentence above -- "those two cells
report N=0" and "pairwise comparisons involving them are excluded" -- describes
a state of the study that lasted one day and was superseded before any analysis
was run. The entry should have carried this note on 1 June. It is added here,
dated, rather than edited into the original, because a deviations log that is
quietly corrected is worth nothing. This is a documentation failure, not a data
one: every published figure re-derives from the frozen CSV.

## Analytical note 001 — 2026-06-01

**Observation:** Replit Agent shipped a data-pipeline CLI when given the
internal_tool_cli specification under a fresh, isolated workspace and an
explicit instruction stem prohibiting pipeline output. The captured
`main.py` defines `cmd_run` and `cmd_schedule` that invoke
`pipeline.runner.run_pipeline` — not the spec's `init / add / list /
export / validate / help` subcommands.

**Why it matters:** This is not contamination or operator error. The
workspace was confirmed clean before capture, and the prompt stem
explicitly forbade pipeline output. Replit's architectural prior
toward pipeline/monorepo scaffolding is sufficient to override an
explicit, contradictory specification — a measurable governance
concern for enterprise adoption.

**Treatment in analysis:** The cell is retained at full weight. The
hallucination metric is the canonical measure of this behaviour.

## Deviation 003 — 2026-06-21

**What changed:** The `human_control` condition was executed as **N = 1 per
spec** — three completed hand-coded sessions (`agent_education_system`,
`data_pipeline`, `internal_tool_cli`) — rather than the pre-registered 30
sessions (3 specs × 10 reps) of 60 minutes each (EXPERIMENT_PROTOCOL.md §6.2).
Sessions were run **to feature-completion** rather than capped at 60 minutes;
all six features of each spec were implemented and verified to execute before
scoring.

**Why:** Hand-coding three specifications is labour-intensive for a single
researcher within the collection window; the agentic conditions parallelise
where the human baseline cannot. The human condition is reframed as a
**single-rep reference point** against the AI distribution, consistent with
Deviation 001's treatment of replay cells as effective singletons.

**Analytical consequence:** `human_control` contributes one observation per
(spec × metric). It is excluded from within-condition variance estimates and
from the ANOVA/Kruskal-Wallis omnibus tests (which remain four-condition,
AI-only), and is reported descriptively in §4.6 as an interpretive floor —
chiefly for `correction_freq`. Keystroke logs are the **concatenation of all
capture segments** per rep (total typing effort including debugging), because
the recorder overwrites its log per invocation. For `agent_education_system`
rep00, an early ~2,133-event coding segment was overwritten before the
segment-archiving procedure existed and is **unrecoverable**; that cell's
`correction_freq` (829/1k) is computed from the 75-event surviving fixing
segment and is reported as a **partial-capture outlier**, not a representative
human authoring rate. The representative human correction rate is taken from
`data_pipeline` (a complete 6,619-event session: 51.8/1k).
